This project presents an AI risk register for Axios Insurance, a fictional digital insurance company operating in Nigeria.

The purpose of the project is to examine how real AI security incidents could affect a specific organisation and turn those lessons into a practical risk management assessment.

The register focuses on three AI systems used by Axios:

* ClaimAssist: an AI system that helps review claims and prepare recommendations.
* UnderwriteAI: an AI system that assists with customer risk assessment.
* SupportAI: an AI customer service assistant.

The project was designed to:

* Identify realistic AI risks within a specific business environment
* Connect each risk to evidence from real AI incidents
* Map risks to recognised AI security frameworks
* Assess likelihood and potential business impact
* Prioritise the most important risks
* Recommend practical controls and mitigations
* Demonstrate how AI risk assessment can support business decision making
